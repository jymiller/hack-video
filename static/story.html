The finding

Go and read the gearing test, not the cover test. The £2.2bn is debt-funded capital spending, and it lands on the one channel that Senior RAR carries at high likelihood and high impact. Senior RAR is the promise with 12.9% of room. Senior ICR — which everything else on the page touches — has 139%.

None of that came from the footage. The footage supplied no ratio, moved no number, and could not have: it told a reader which of two tests to go and open. The filing that settles it arrives months later. That gap is the whole product.

And the discipline is the other half. The water outage led every bulletin and was worth nothing. A model said otherwise and a person overruled it. A system that reacts to the loudest footage gets this exactly backwards.
