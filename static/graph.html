Why this can't be cheated. Both arrows end at the risk — nothing leads out of it. So you cannot start at a news clip and walk forward to a loan promise; there is no path. The only way across is to start at the loan, which declares its own exposures, and read backwards. A risk holds no number, no unit and no threshold, so there is nothing on it for a clip to contaminate. The footage can move where you look. It can never move what you read.
