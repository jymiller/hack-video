The new bit

The model guesses; a human decides

Asking a model "which covenant could this news event threaten?" is the interesting question and the dangerous one. So the graph separates the two acts rather than blending them.

MAY_AFFECT is a proposal until a human signs it

The model writes the edge with asserted_by:'model' and status:'proposed'. It stays dashed, and no query that computes anything will read it. A human moves it to validated or rejected, and their name and timestamp go on the edge. Both states are visible — a rejected guess is kept, not deleted, because the record of what the model got wrong is the most honest thing on screen.

Why this is worth demoing

Every other team will show a model asserting something. Showing the model assert, then showing a human overrule it — with both preserved and distinguishable — is the part a credit audience will actually believe.
