On the word "material"

A breach is material when it changes what a lender may do about it. That is a question about the numbers in a filing — never a question about what was on the news.

So the footage is a witness, not a signatory. It can tell you where to look, months before the paperwork arrives. It can never tell you a breach occurred. Keeping those two things apart is the entire architecture.
