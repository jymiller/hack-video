# The counter-example — when evidence *does* reach a covenant

*Research finding, 30 July. Sourced to primary documents. See the "What is corrected" section
before reusing any number from [`02-the-corpus.md`](02-the-corpus.md).*

The demo proves a negative: news footage never reaches a loan covenant. That negative is only
interesting if the system can be shown reaching one when the evidence is admissible. This
document is that counter-example, and it is stronger than the repo assumed — because the
document that settles it is **a compliance certificate**, the exact artifact the fictional
Meridian corpus faked.

---

## 1 · Verdict first

**The COVID covenant waiver happened. Twice. Put it on stage.**

The repo's claim — lenders waived Senior ICR and Senior RAR around August 2021 — is **true in
substance, imprecise in date, and incomplete**: there were two waivers, not one, and the
earlier one covers the worse breach.

**But one headline number in the repo is wrong and must not go on stage.** The "~£2bn pre-tax
loss for 2020" is **Heathrow's** figure, not Gatwick's. Details in §5.

---

## 2 · The primary document

**Gatwick Airport Limited and Ivy Bidco Limited, Compliance Certificate for the Calculation
Date 31 December 2021.** Addressed to Deutsche Trustee Company Limited as Borrower Security
Trustee. Dated **7 March 2022**. Signed by **Stewart Wingate, Chief Executive Officer** and
**Jim Butler, Chief Financial Officer**.

> [Compliance certificate December 2021](https://www.gatwickairport.com/on/demandware.static/-/Sites-Gatwick-Library/default/dw6bbd5197/images/Corporate-PDFs/Reports%20financial%20/2022/Compliance%20certificate%20December%202021.pdf)

Paragraph 6(a), verbatim:

> "a Default has occurred and is continuing and the following steps are being taken in
> connection with such Default: … the Group issued a request for a covenant waiver and
> amendment of certain terms under the financing documents, which was approved by the
> Qualifying Borrower Secured Creditors and documented under the **Amendment and Waiver
> Agreement dated 8 September 2021**. This includes: a) a waiver in respect of any Default
> relating to Senior ICR and Senior RAR levels in respect of the calculation dates falling on
> **December 2021 and June 2022** and b) a temporary amendment (until **June 2024**) to the
> calculation of the Senior RAR to replace the April 2021 to March 2022 EBITDA in such
> calculation with the average of the 2017, 2018 and 2019 financial years corresponding to
> each relevant calendar quarter…"

This is the whole chain on one page: the governing agreement named (Common Terms Agreement
dated 15 February 2011), the breach admitted, the waiver identified by date, the covenants
named, the test dates named, and two officers signing it.

**This is a filing, not an article about a filing.**

## 3 · The two waivers

### Waiver 1 — the 22 September 2020 Amendments

Source: **Gatwick Funding Limited Base Prospectus, March 2021**, pp. 12–13, "Financial
covenants and waiver", cross-referenced internally as the "22 September 2020 Amendments".

> [Gatwick Funding Limited Base Prospectus March 2021](https://www.gatwickairport.com/on/demandware.static/-/Sites-Gatwick-Library/default/dw65b113d4/images/Corporate-PDFs/Reports%20financial%20/Prospectus/Gatwick_Funding_Limited_Base_Prospectus_March_2021.pdf)

> "To mitigate the impact of the Covid-19 global pandemic, in September 2020, GAL entered into
> an amendment and waiver agreement…"

What was agreed:

- a waiver of any Default in respect of the financial ratios at the Calculation Dates falling
  on **31 December 2020 and 30 June 2021**
- an amendment to the Transfer RAB calculation for Calculation Dates from (excluding) June
  2021 to (including) June 2023 — Relevant EBITDA for each quarter in the 12 months from
  1 April 2020 replaced with the average of the same quarters in 2017, 2018, 2019
- waiver of certain other technical Defaults arising from government COVID action
- approval to issue up to **£300,000,000** of unsecured commercial paper under the CCFF
- a waiver of certain consequences of the occurrence of a Trigger Event

And what it cost Gatwick — the part that matters for the demo, because it shows a waiver is
priced, not free:

- quarterly information packages to secured creditors, Sept 2020 → June 2021
- a liquidity test: if Available Cash minus Required Expenditure falls below **£225m**, a
  remedial plan is required; below **£150m**, failure to provide or execute that plan is
  itself a **Loan Event of Default**
- **no Restricted Payments** before the Calculation Date on 31 December 2021, and thereafter
  a tighter Senior RAR test for distributions (0.60 to June 2022, then 0.65, adjusted basis)

### Waiver 2 — the Amendment and Waiver Agreement dated 8 September 2021

Primary: the compliance certificate quoted in §2. Corroborating, and useful for the £2.8bn
figure:

> [VINCI, "London Gatwick Airport: waiver agreement on financial covenants", 8 September 2021](https://www.vinci.com/vinci.nsf/en/press-releases/pages/20210908-0830.htm)

VINCI (50.01% shareholder) states the waiver relates to **£2.8bn of reference net debt** at
end-June 2021, that Gatwick "will not be required to comply with the Senior ICR … and Senior
RAR … at the next two testing points, i.e. at end-December 2021 and end-June 2022", and that
the debt-ratio calculation is amended until June 2024.

**Be precise on stage: this is a corporate press release from the parent, not the agreement.**
The agreement itself is named and dated in the compliance certificate; we do not hold it.

## 4 · The covenant, the thresholds, the tiers, the consequence

Both covenants sit in the **Common Terms Agreement dated 15 February 2011** and are tested
**bi-annually at 30 June and 31 December**. The thresholds are unchanged between the March
2019 and March 2021 prospectuses — the same test governed both the drone and the pandemic.

| Covenant | Trigger Event | Loan Event of Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Senior ICR** — minimum interest cover | **< 1.50** | **≤ 1.10** |
| **Senior RAR** — max net debt ÷ RAB | **> 0.70** | **> 0.85** |

Two tiers, two different consequences:

- **Trigger Event** → cash lock-up. Prohibition of distributions to shareholders, additional
  ratio disclosure in Investor Reports, escalating consequences if it runs 12 months or more.
- **Loan Event of Default** → acceleration risk. But with a 30-day cure: no Loan Event of
  Default occurs if, within 30 days of the Calculation Date, GAL procures Additional SP
  Contributions applied in prepayment of Senior Debt such that Senior RAR < 0.85 and Senior
  ICR > 1.1.

### The actual ratios

| Calculation Date | Senior ICR | Senior RAR | Where it sits |
|---|---|---|---|
| 31 Mar 2017 | 3.96 | 0.51 | clear |
| 31 Mar 2018 | 3.59 | 0.61 | clear |
| **31 Mar 2019** | **2.93** | **0.59** | **clear — this is the drone year** |
| 31 Dec 2019 | 3.15 | 0.60 | clear |
| **31 Dec 2020** | **(1.29)** | **0.94** | **both through the Event of Default level** |
| **31 Dec 2021** | **(1.49)** | **0.81** | **ICR through Default; RAR through Trigger only** |

Read the last two rows carefully, because the honest version is more dramatic than "the
covenants were breached":

- **Senior ICR went negative.** −1.29 and −1.49 against a floor of 1.10. Not a near miss —
  the interest cover ratio inverted, because cash generated from operations was negative.
- **December 2020 breached both at Event of Default level.** RAR 0.94 against a 0.85 ceiling.
  (The prospectus notes this 0.94 is calculated *without* the agreed adjustments.)
- **December 2021 is the more precise story.** RAR 0.81 broke the **0.70 Trigger** but stayed
  *inside* the **0.85 Default** ceiling. ICR at −1.49 was through the Default floor. So on the
  same test date, one covenant was a lock-up breach and the other was a default-level breach.

The compliance certificate also confirms the lock-up was live: paragraph 6(d), "no Restricted
Payments have been made since the date of the delivery of the immediately previous Compliance
Certificate."

**Absent the waiver, December 2021 was a Loan Event of Default on Senior ICR.** That is the
sentence the counter-example exists to earn.

### The forecast columns

The same certificate carries the recovery, which is what a waiver is *for*:

| | 2022 | 2023 | 2024 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Forecast Senior ICR | 2.82 | 2.79 | 3.04 |
| Forecast Senior RAR | 0.62 | 0.56 | 0.57 |

Working at 31 December 2021: Senior Debt net of cash **£2,859.3m** ÷ RAB **£3,549.2m** = 0.81.

## 5 · What is corrected

Ruthlessly separating verified from inferred, per the brief.

### ❌ REFUTED — "~£2bn pre-tax loss for 2020"

**This is Heathrow's number.** Heathrow reported a pre-tax loss of **£2.01bn** for the 12
months to 31 December 2020.

Gatwick's Security Group (Ivy Holdco Limited consolidated) reported:

| | 2020 | 2021 |
|---|---|---|
| Loss before tax | **£525.9m** | **£368.7m** |
| Loss after tax | **£465.5m** | **£370.6m** |

Sources: Ivy Holdco Limited Annual Report FY2021 (2020 as comparative), and the March 2021
base prospectus — "The Security Group has reported a loss of £465.5 million for the year ended
31 December 2020".

Even 2020 and 2021 *combined* is £894.6m before tax. **Do not say £2bn.**

### ⚠️ IMPRECISE — "August 2021"

August 2021 is when Gatwick *entered discussions* with lenders. The **Amendment and Waiver
Agreement is dated 8 September 2021**. And the repo omits the **22 September 2020** waiver
entirely — which covers the worse breach (Dec 2020, RAR 0.94).

### ✅ VERIFIED — "£2.8bn of reference net debt"

VINCI's figure for end-June 2021. Corroborated by the compliance certificate: Senior Debt net
of cash £2,859.3m at 31 December 2021.

### ✅ VERIFIED — "Senior ICR 3.59×, down from 3.96×"

Gatwick Airport Limited ARFS March 2018: "The Senior ICR for the year ended 31 March 2018 was
3.59 (2017: 3.96)." Senior RAR 0.61 (2017: 0.51).

The run-of-show line — *"Senior RAR is capped at 0.70 and stands at 0.61 — 12.9% of headroom.
Senior ICR has 139%"* — is arithmetically correct ((0.70−0.61)/0.70 = 12.9%;
(3.59−1.50)/1.50 = 139%) but is the **31 March 2018** vintage. Label it, or update to 31 March
2019 (0.59 and 2.93).

## 6 · The drone contrast — verified, and better than claimed

The repo asserts the drone had no covenant consequence. **That is now proven from the borrower
group's own accounts, not inferred from the absence of news.**

**Ivy Holdco Limited, Annual Report and Financial Statements for the year ended 31 March
2019** — the financial year containing the 19–21 December 2018 shutdown.

> [Annual Report and Results March 2019](https://www.gatwickairport.com/on/demandware.static/-/Sites-Gatwick-Library/default/dwf3ddc233/images/Corporate-PDFs/Reports%20financial%20/2019/Annual_Report_and_Results_March_2019.pdf)

> "All financial covenants have been tested and complied with as at 31 March 2019 (2018: all
> covenants tested and complied with)."

| Covenant | 31 March 2019 | Trigger | Default |
|---|---|---|---|
| Minimum interest cover ratio ("Senior ICR") | 2.93 | < 1.50 | < 1.10 |
| Maximum net indebtedness to the total regulatory asset base ("Senior RAR") | 0.59 | > 0.70 | > 0.85 |

**And the word "drone" appears zero times in the 103-page report.** Not in the strategic
report, not in the risk section, not in exceptional items, not in the notes. The most
televised event in the airport's history is absent from the accounts of the year in which it
happened.

That is the demo's thesis stated by Gatwick's own auditors' filing rather than by us.

### Where the drone *does* appear in a filing

In the **Gatwick Funding Limited Base Prospectus, March 2019**, as a risk factor titled "Drone
Risk" — and its numbers do not match the news:

| | News figure (repo) | Gatwick's own prospectus |
|---|---|---|
| Closure duration | ~33–36 hours | **31 hours** |
| Passengers | ~140,000 | **164,000 fewer passengers** |
| Sightings | — | **115, between 19 and 23 December** |

**This discrepancy is a gift to the argument.** The same event carries one number on
television and a different number in the bond document. The covenant world uses the second.
The prospectus discusses the drone purely as forward-looking risk — no waiver, no Trigger
Event, no ratio impact, no consequence.

### ⚠️ The £1.4m is news-only and contested

- **£1.4m** — *The Guardian*, 18 June 2019, "Gatwick drone disruption cost airport just
  £1.4m". **It does not appear in Gatwick's annual report.**
- **~£15m** — airport-technology's feature says £15m "will come directly from Gatwick's
  pockets", unattributed.
- **~£50m** to airlines — extrapolated by *The Independent* from easyJet's £15m. Not primary.
- **£5m** on counter-drone technology; **~£886k** Sussex Police investigation.

So the cost-to-Gatwick figure exists **only in journalism, and journalists disagree about it
by an order of magnitude.** If you say "£1.4m" on stage, attribute it to the Guardian and
say the filing does not carry the number. Better: **do not lead with the cost at all.** Lead
with 2.93 and 0.59 against 1.50 and 0.70 — those come from the filing, and they are the
numbers the covenant actually reads.

## 7 · What we hold, and what we do not

**Hold (primary, downloaded, verified):**

- Compliance certificate, Calculation Date 31 December 2021, signed CEO + CFO — the waiver
  named and dated, the Default admitted, ICR −1.49, RAR 0.81
- Ivy Holdco Annual Report FY2021 — covenant table, waiver note, loss before tax
- Ivy Holdco Annual Report FY March 2019 — drone-year covenant table, "tested and complied
  with", zero mentions of "drone"
- GAL ARFS March 2018 — 3.59 / 0.61, and 2017 comparatives 3.96 / 0.51
- Gatwick Funding base prospectuses March 2019 and March 2021 — thresholds, tiers,
  Event of Default definitions, the 22 September 2020 Amendments, "Drone Risk"

**Do not hold:**

- The Amendment and Waiver Agreement dated 8 September 2021 itself
- The 2020 STID Proposal / consent solicitation document
- An RNS announcement of either waiver
- A rating agency action on the waiver

The evidence is **filing-grade documents that describe and reference the agreement**, signed
by officers, not the agreement. Say that if asked. It is more than enough — a compliance
certificate stating "a Default has occurred and is continuing" is the strongest artifact in
the corpus.

## 8 · What this buys the demo

The existing demo shows video → **stops**. This adds a second lane that does not stop:

```
compliance certificate → Senior ICR = −1.49 → threshold 1.10 → Loan Event of Default
                       → waived, 8 Sept 2021, by the Qualifying Borrower Secured Creditors
```

Same borrower. Same graph. Same two covenant nodes — `cta_senior_icr`, `cta_senior_rar` —
that read **0 · 0** from the video. From the filing they read −1.49 and 0.81, with a threshold,
a tier, and a consequence attached.

**The contrast is the product.** News cannot reach the covenant, and the system reports the
zero honestly. A filing reaches it, and the system carries it all the way through. The system
is not incapable — it is *discriminating*, and the counter-example is what proves the
difference.

The line that earns it:

> *"The airport was on every news channel on earth for three days, and it is not in the
> accounts for that year — not once. What is in the accounts is an interest cover ratio of
> minus one point four nine, against a floor of one point one, and two officers signing to say
> a default has occurred and is continuing."*
