### The freeze arithmetic

This is a **two-gate event**, which is unusual for us, and the earlier gate binds.

- Submission at 16:00, treated as a submitted artifact → 16:00 − 90 min = **14:30**
- Demo at 17:15, treated as a live appearance → 17:15 − 2 hrs = 15:15

**14:30 stands.** It is the earlier of the two, and a freeze that only protects the later
gate protects nothing. Getting this wrong costs 45 minutes of slack, and the interval
between the last working change and the judged moment is the strongest relationship in this
desk's entire record — measured at roughly zero, +4h, −24min and −27sec across four events,
of which only the positive one won.

**Actual build window: 11:00 → 14:30 = 3 hours 30 minutes.** Less lunch.

## What they ask for

> "Create a video agent that can ingest raw video, understand it across vision, audio, and
> speech with TwelveLabs, and write what it finds into a Neo4j context graph: entities,
> scenes, tags, and the relationships between them."

No video, dataset or sample corpus is provided. We choose the material — which makes the
choice of video the highest-leverage decision available, and it is covered in
[`02-the-corpus.md`](02-the-corpus.md).
